Who We Are

India's pioneering private gold explorer & developer

Established in 1994, Geomysore Services (India) Pvt. Ltd. (GMSI) is one of India's first private exploration companies, focused on the discovery and development of precious and base metal resources. We believe in responsible and sustainable business practices that create lasting value for shareholders, communities and the nation.

Backed by experienced international promoters and partners — including Australian Indian Resources, Deccan Gold Mines and Thriveni Earthmovers & Infra — we combine global technical standards with deep local expertise.

  • Over 35,000 sq. km of ground explored across eight Indian states
  • JORC- and NI 43-101-compliant resource estimation and feasibility standards
  • Integrated capability from grassroots exploration to mine development and processing

Latest milestones

2026 · Production

Jonnagiri enters commercial gold production

Following final pre-production works, commercial operations have commenced at Jonnagiri — initial output of around 500 kg of gold a year, rising towards 1,000 kg annually at full capacity.

2025 · Environment

Environmental Clearance granted for processing plant

The MoEF&CC Expert Appraisal Committee granted Environmental Clearance for the Jonnagiri gold ore processing plant, completing the project's major environmental approvals.

2025 · Operations

Consent to Operate received from APPCB

The Andhra Pradesh Pollution Control Board issued Consent to Operate for 0.4 MTPA mining and 0.3 MTPA ore processing — clearing the path to full-scale production.
