Gold that gives back

We believe a mine should leave its host communities stronger than it found them — through water, education, healthcare, infrastructure and opportunity.

Our Approach

Three pillars of responsibility

Environmental Conservation

Mining and processing designed to minimise environmental impact — from water stewardship and dust suppression to progressive rehabilitation of mined land.

Community Development

Long-term investment in the villages that host our operations — Jonnagiri, Erragudi and Pagadirayi — across water, education, health and livelihoods.

Ethical Mining

Transparent engagement, fair employment, full statutory compliance and international reporting standards in everything we do.

Flagship Programme

Water security for our host villages

In a drought-prone corner of Kurnool district, reliable water changes everything. Before the first gold was poured, GMSI invested in securing water for the people of Jonnagiri, Erragudi and Pagadirayi.

  • 40 wells drilled across the project villages
  • 10 yielding bore wells equipped with pumps in Jonnagiri and Pagadirayi
  • Water supplied to roughly 70% of the local population
  • Continuous drinking water arranged for all 3 villages surrounding the mining lease

On the Ground

Community programmes

Our CSR initiatives are practical and local — built with the community, for the community.

Education

Education programmes for children in the surrounding villages — supporting schools, learning materials and access for every child.

Healthcare

Healthcare services and health camps that bring medical attention closer to families in remote host communities.

Infrastructure

Village infrastructure development — roads, water systems and public facilities that serve residents long beyond the life of the mine.

Skill Development

Training local people for skilled roles in mining, processing and services — turning a gold project into local careers.

NGOs, government agencies and local institutions — let's build programmes that outlast the ore body.
