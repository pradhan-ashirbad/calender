How We Explore

Science-led discovery

We identify potential gold-bearing deposits with precision and responsibility, integrating three complementary disciplines.

Geological Surveys

Detailed mapping of lithology, structure and alteration — building the geological framework that vectors towards mineralised systems.

Geochemical Sampling

Soil, stream-sediment and rock-chip geochemistry that traces pathfinder elements back to their golden source.

Geophysical Surveys

Magnetic, electrical and gravity methods that image structure beneath cover — finding what the surface cannot show.

From Permit to Production

A disciplined exploration pipeline

Exploration succeeds through discipline: regional reconnaissance narrows to prospecting, prospecting to drilling, and drilling to mining — exactly the path that took Jonnagiri from a regional target to India's first private gold mine.

  • Reconnaissance Permits — 18,490 sq. km of ground secured under 25 RP applications
  • Prospecting Licences — 39 PL applications over 819.2 sq. km, with key grants around the Kolar Gold Field
  • Mining Leases — 7 ML applications over 33.4 sq. km; the Jonnagiri ML granted and now in production
