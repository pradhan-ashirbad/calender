The Jonnagiri Gold Project

India's first private-sector gold mine with an integrated processing facility in 70 years — Tuggali Mandal, Kurnool district, Andhra Pradesh.

Executive Summary

Construction-ready, cash-generative, nationally significant

Jonnagiri is one of the most well-developed gold projects in the country, offering strong cash flows over the next 8–10 years. An open-pit mining operation and the simple metallurgy of the gold ore make the project highly attractive.

The project spans the villages of Jonnagiri, Erragudi and Pagadirayi, with a granted Mining Lease of 597.82 ha valid until 2063 and an NI 43-101 compliant feasibility study. Over ₹400 crore has been invested to develop the mine and its integrated processing facility.

  • Granted Mining Lease — 597.82 ha, valid until 2063
  • NI 43-101 compliant feasibility study completed
  • All major statutory approvals in place — EC, CFE, CTO and canal water allocation
  • Target output of 750 kg of gold per year, scaling towards 1,000 kg

Resources & Geology

Four mineralised blocks, one proven system

Gold mineralisation within the Mining Lease is distributed across four blocks — East, West, South and North. Detailed exploration in the East and West Blocks underpins the current resource, with significant upside remaining.

East Block

The most advanced block and site of the current open pit. Detailed drilling and rigorous internal evaluation indicate 6.8 tonnes of mineable gold to a total depth of 180 m, supporting a mine life of 8–9 years.

West Block

Detailed exploration alongside the East Block contributes to the project's 361,000 oz JORC Mineral Resource (Indicated and Inferred), of which roughly 271,000 oz is open-pittable.

North & South Blocks

Earlier-stage mineralised blocks offering clear brownfield growth. Ongoing exploration targets an upside of up to 42.5 tonnes (1.5 Moz) of gold across the lease.

Mining

Modern open-pit operations

Mining in the East Block is an open-pit operation using conventional drilling and blasting, planned from pit-optimisation outputs targeting 495 ktpa at a 0.6 g/t cut-off grade. The approved Mining Plan permits operations at 0.4 MTPA.

  • Bench heights of 7.5 m with 10 m operating widths
  • Pit progresses systematically from north to south
  • Safe, efficient extraction designed to maximise recovery and minimise environmental impact
  • Mine development and operations with partner Thriveni Earthmovers & Infra

Mineral Processing

From ore to gold, on one site

Jonnagiri's integrated 0.3 MTPA processing facility turns ore into refined gold on site — the simple metallurgy of the deposit keeps recoveries high and operating costs competitive.

Our state-of-the-art pilot plant serves as the bridge between exploration and full-scale production, allowing the team to test, refine and optimise gold extraction processes before they are scaled.

  • Integrated mine-to-gold flowsheet with simple, proven metallurgy
  • Pilot plant for continuous metallurgical test work and optimisation
  • Environmental Clearance for the processing plant granted in 2025

Compliance

Statutory clearances

Jonnagiri holds all major government approvals required for full-scale mining and processing.

Mining Lease granted over 597.82 ha in favour of Geomysore Services (India) Pvt. Ltd. — approved by the Government of India and valid until 2063.

Environmental Clearance for mining operations, with the EC for the gold ore processing plant granted by the MoEF&CC Expert Appraisal Committee (Non-Coal Mining Sector) in 2025 following public hearing.

CFE and CTO issued by the Andhra Pradesh Pollution Control Board, permitting 0.4 MTPA mining and 0.3 MTPA ore processing operations.

Approval for water withdrawal from a nearby canal secures the project's process-water requirements without burdening community sources.
