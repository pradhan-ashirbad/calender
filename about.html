Our Story

Founded in 1994, proving India's gold potential ever since

Geomysore Services (India) Pvt. Ltd. was established in Bengaluru in 1994 by experienced Australian promoters, at the very dawn of private mineral exploration in India. Our mandate has remained constant: the systematic exploration and development of India's precious and base metal resources.

Over thirty years, GMSI has explored more than 35,000 sq. km of geologically promising ground and made several discoveries of gold and precious metals. That patient, science-led approach culminated in the Jonnagiri Gold Project — the first private-sector gold mine with an integrated processing facility to be developed in India in 70 years.

We operate to international standards — JORC and NI 43-101 compliant resource estimation, rigorous feasibility studies and transparent governance — while remaining deeply rooted in the communities that host our work.

Milestones

Our journey

  1. 1994

    Geomysore is born

    Incorporated in Bengaluru by experienced Australian promoters — among the very first private mineral exploration companies in India.

  2. 1990s–2000s

    India-wide exploration

    Reconnaissance Permit applications covering 18,490 sq. km; systematic exploration across the Kolar, Ramagiri, Shimoga, Chitradurga, Nellore and Veligallu schist belts of southern India and the Mahakoshal, Sakoli, Kotri and Pathalgaon belts of central India.

  3. 2010s

    Jonnagiri takes shape

    Detailed drilling delineates the Jonnagiri deposit in Kurnool district, Andhra Pradesh. A Mining Lease over 597.82 ha is granted (valid to 2063) and an NI 43-101 compliant feasibility study is completed.

  4. 2023–2025

    From permits to plant

    Environmental Clearance, Consent for Establishment and Consent to Operate secured; over ₹400 crore invested in building the open-pit mine and integrated 0.3 MTPA processing facility.

  5. 2025–2026

    India's gold renaissance begins

    Commissioning and first commercial gold production at Jonnagiri — the first private-sector gold mine with an integrated processing facility in India in 70 years.
